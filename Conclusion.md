This paper proposes a study evaluating how well GPT-4o mini performs at zero-shot unit test generation for Python functions of varying cyclomatic complexity. Test quality will be measured along four dimensions — branch coverage, mutation score, correctness, and generation time — with GPT-4o mini's results benchmarked against Randoop. The goal is to offer concrete evidence on whether a lightweight LLM like GPT-4o mini is practical for automated unit testing, and to give future work on LLM-based software testing a clearer starting point.
